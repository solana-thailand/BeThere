Low risk, but it is an access-control latency choice — note it in the commit. **TTL raise declined by the owner (2026-09-24):** revocation stays at 60 s. A copy picked up from KV late therefore expires no later than the KV entry, so revocation stays ≤ 60 s from the Sheets read. It saves the KV read on repeat authed requests in a warm isolate.
