# Frequently asked questions

## Attendees

### What is BeThere?

A deposit-backed event check-in platform. Attendees put down a deposit, show up, get scanned, and get the whole deposit back, plus a compressed NFT badge on Solana.

### Why do I need to sign in?

A deposit is real money, so every registration belongs to a verified account. Sign in with Google or a Solana wallet; there is no guest registration.

### Do attendees need a crypto wallet?

No. Checking in and paying by PromptPay work on any phone. A wallet is only needed to pay a USDC deposit or to claim the NFT badge.

### How does the deposit work?

Organizers set a deposit amount (e.g., 500 THB). PromptPay deposits are off-chain: the organizer transfers it back within 7 days after the event. If you can't make it, tell the organizer before the cut-off; the organizer sets the rule. USDC deposits sit in a Solana escrow (on devnet for now) and you claim them back after the event ends.

### Where is my ticket?

Sign in on the home page. Under Your events, tap Show ticket for the QR code, or open the full ticket. The QR appears once your deposit is verified; show it at the entrance and staff scan it in a couple of seconds.

### When can I claim my badge?

After staff check you in. Your ticket then leads to the claim page. The claim link works for a limited time after check-in, so claim it soon.

### Can I cancel?

There is no cancel button yet. Tell the organizer, who can cancel your registration. What happens to a PromptPay deposit is in the deposit answer above.

### Is it only for crypto events?

It works for any event — meetups, workshops, conferences, hackathons. The blockchain part runs behind the scenes; attendees don't need to know anything about crypto.

### What do you do with my data?

Only what running the event needs. You can stop marketing emails or ask for deletion yourself. [Your data →](/data-privacy) · [Privacy policy →](/privacy)

## Organizers

### How do I host an event?

Organizer access is by invitation for now: join the waitlist on the home page and we will reach out. After that you create the event, its deposit and its registration form in the admin.

### How does capacity work?

Set separate in-person and online caps. The event page shows the seats left and says full when none are left.

### How does check-in work at the door?

Your staff open the scanner on any phone, no app needed, and scan each ticket's QR code. Walk-ins can be registered on the spot, and a live dashboard shows arrivals.

### How are deposits settled?

PromptPay deposits reach you off-chain; you return them within 7 days after the event, or the attendee keeps them as credit for a next event. USDC deposits stay in the Solana escrow (on devnet for now) until the attendee claims them back.
